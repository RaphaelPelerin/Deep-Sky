Why Deep Sky

A whole sky in one line of code.

Built for landing pages, portfolios and dashboards that deserve a little magic, without paying for it in performance.

Off the main thread

Shader and particles run in a Web Worker on an OffscreenCanvas. Your clicks, requests and renders stay instant.

Adapts to the device

It measures its own frame time and lowers resolution, then frame rate, on slower GPUs. It backs off while your page is busy.

Feels alive

A light follows your cursor, stars swirl into constellations, and clicks send shockwaves through the nebula.

Fully themeable

Seven presets, every color and layer configurable, and a smooth crossfade between dark and light themes.

Works everywhere

Vanilla JS, React, Vue, Svelte, Angular, or a plain <deep-sky> tag. TypeScript types included.

Polite by default

Respects reduced motion, pauses off-screen and in background tabs, and falls back gracefully when WebGL is missing.

Anatomy

Seven layers, all yours.

Hover a layer to see it alone. Each one can be tuned or switched off, live.

New · 3D

Pick a shape or drop in your own glTF model. The sky lights it: the glow follows your cursor, the aurora traces its edges, the nebula shines in its reflections, and every click sends a shockwave through it.
